Frequently asked questions

What is a debrid service and do I need one?

A debrid service (such as Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, or TorBox) is a third-party cloud service that downloads torrents on its servers and gives you a direct HTTPS link. You bring your own debrid account and API token. Lidhra does not provide debrid accounts or content.

On the App Store version, a debrid account is required because direct torrenting is not available. On the desktop direct-download version, you can choose to torrent locally without a debrid account.

How do I find my debrid API token?

Log in to your debrid provider's website and look for an API or settings page. For example, on Real-Debrid go to My Account → API to find your API token. Copy it and paste it into Lidhra when prompted.

Is there a free trial?

The direct-download version (macOS, Windows, Linux via Ko-fi) includes a 7-day free trial. No account is needed to start the trial. After the trial, a one-time licence purchase unlocks the app permanently.

The App Store version (iPhone, iPad, Mac) has no trial. It is a one-time purchase at checkout. Once purchased, the app is fully unlocked with no additional purchases or subscriptions required.

How do I activate my licence after buying on Ko-fi?

Open Lidhra and choose "Activate with your Ko-fi email." Enter the email address you used for the Ko-fi purchase. The app contacts the licence service, verifies your purchase, and activates automatically. Your licence covers up to 3 devices.

What is included in the App Store version?

The App Store version includes debrid cloud transfers (resolving magnets through your debrid provider over HTTPS). It works on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Updates are managed by the App Store, and Family Sharing is supported. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

The direct torrent engine (local peer-to-peer) is only available in the direct-download desktop version.

What data does Lidhra collect?

None. Lidhra has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no tracking. Your debrid credentials are stored locally on your device and sent only to the debrid provider you chose. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Which platforms are supported?

Lidhra is available on macOS, Windows, Linux (AppImage, deb, Flatpak), iOS (App Store), and Android. A watchOS companion shows transfer status at a glance.

My downloads are not starting. What should I check?

First, verify that your debrid API token is correct and that your debrid account has an active premium subscription. Check your internet connection. If you are on a restrictive network (corporate, school), HTTPS traffic to your debrid provider may be blocked.

If the problem persists, email info@peterdsp.dev with your platform, app version, and a description of the issue.

System requirements

  • macOS: macOS 12 Monterey or later (Universal binary)
  • Windows: Windows 10 version 1803 or later (x64 / ARM)
  • Linux: Ubuntu 22.04+, Fedora 38+, or equivalent with WebKitGTK
  • iOS / iPadOS: iOS 16 or later
  • Android: Android 8.0 or later
